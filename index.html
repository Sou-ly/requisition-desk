Autofill

Application forms live on Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever and Workday — other people's domains, which this page cannot reach into or embed. So the filling happens where the form is: a bookmarklet carries your profile into the tab and completes the fields there.

Your profile

Stays in this browser, and nothing here is sent anywhere. Your answers are written into the bookmarklet below, so drag a fresh copy onto your bar whenever you change something here.

The bookmarklet

One install, then one click per application. It reads the labels on whatever form is open, fills every field it can match, ticks the work-authorisation and sponsorship answers, and leaves a panel listing what it filled and what still needs you.

⌁ Fill this application
  1. Show your browser's bookmarks bar — Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B.
  2. Drag the black button onto the bar. If dragging out of this page is blocked, press Copy the code instead, then make a new bookmark by hand and paste it as the URL.
  3. Open a job's application form, then click the bookmark. Nothing is submitted — read the form, fix what it flagged, and press the real submit button yourself.

Handled by name: Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever and Workday — — of the — roles. Everything else falls back to reading the form's own labels, which carries most of the remainder. Résumé uploads are a browser security boundary: no script can attach your file, so the bookmarklet highlights the upload box instead.
